## [Secondary Category: game-developers] Proprietary Game Design and Mechanics

Beyond supplying content, ELK Studios functions as a game developer that designs its own math models and mechanics in-house rather than licensing or adapting third-party systems [#18]. The company describes a three-stage development process moving from concept and theme, through in-house artwork and sound, to a proprietary mathematical model built using statistical modelling and cloud computing [#16].

This in-house approach produces mechanics unique to ELK's catalogue: Nitro Reels and Nitro Multipliers in the Nitropolis series, the CollectR™ collection mechanic introduced in Pirots 4, and game-specific systems such as the Feature Row used across multiple titles including Nightmaker, Ashoka and Deadeye. Rather than reusing a single templated engine, each release combines new symbol behaviors — walking wilds, symbol splits, orb systems, feature rows — into a distinct ruleset.

The studio also runs its own quality assurance through fairness testing: games use certified Random Number Generators verified by independent labs before release [#16]. This design discipline is what distinguishes ELK Studios as a game developer from aggregator-style suppliers that resell third-party catalogues, reinforcing its identity as a build-not-buy content studio.

## [Tag: x-iter bonus buy] X-iter — Proprietary Bonus Buy System

X-iter™ is ELK Studios' branded bonus-buy feature, giving players five predefined game modes that adjust stake and feature access within a single title [#2]. Modes typically range from a lower-multiplier stake that improves the odds of triggering a bonus round to a high-multiplier stake that grants direct entry into a super bonus with guaranteed enhancements — for example, a 3x bet mode with an increased chance to trigger the bonus, up to a 500x bet mode with a guaranteed feature outcome [#2]. Exact configurations vary by title: Nightmaker's top X-iter mode guarantees a jumping wild in its super bonus, while Nitropolis 4's guarantees Max Rows and Both Ways features active from the start [#2].

The X-iter bonus buy system is deployed across the large majority of ELK's active catalogue, appearing consistently on game pages as a standard purchasable option rather than a one-off promotional mechanic [#2]. The feature won Game Innovation of the Year at the 5 Star Starlet Awards [#19].

## [Tag: collectr mechanic] CollectR — The Pirots Series Collection Mechanic

The CollectR mechanic is a proprietary system introduced with Pirots 4 and carried through its Frozen Fury and Inferno expansions, layering a symbol-collection structure onto an expanding 6×6 game grid [#18]. Unlike ELK's earlier Pirots instalments, Pirots 4 pairs CollectR with a time-based evolution structure, in which an in-game countdown unlocks a follow-on version of the game once it elapses [#18].

CollectR is specific to the Pirots 4 sub-series rather than deployed studio-wide, distinguishing it from cross-catalogue systems like X-iter. Each expansion — the base Pirots 4, Frozen Fury and Inferno — retains the same CollectR foundation while introducing new antagonists and coin-game variants layered on top of it [#18].
