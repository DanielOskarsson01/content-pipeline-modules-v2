## [Overview] Nolimit City — B2B Slot Provider Built Around High-Volatility Online Slot Games

Nolimit City is a Malta-licensed B2B iGaming software provider supplying high-volatility online slot games directly to operators in regulated markets worldwide [#1][#2]. Established in 2014, the company operates with teams based in Malta, Sweden, India and Romania [#2], and became part of Evolution Group in 2022 [#2], gaining distribution through Evolution's One Stop Shop integration platform [#2].

Nolimit City Limited holds an active B2B gambling supplier licence from the Malta Gaming Authority, MGA/B2B/299/2015, issued in 2018 [#1]. Its catalogue is built around proprietary mathematical mechanics — including xNudge®, xWays® and xSplit® — combined with a stated design philosophy centred on extreme volatility and large maximum-win potential [#2][#6]. Titles such as Tombstone, San Quentin and Mental illustrate this approach, with volatility and payout structure positioned by the company as its core commercial differentiator [#2][#9].

The company has expanded into regulated markets through direct commercial partnerships, including agreements with SuperSport in Croatia [#3] and Apollo Games in the Czech Republic [#4], alongside a simultaneous launch across six US states via Evolution [#2]. As a B2B iGaming software provider, Nolimit City is described in its own market-entry announcements as an "award-winning software provider" [#4] — a claim not independently verified in the sourced material. Its distinguishing feature is less catalogue breadth than a recognisable house style of high-volatility slots and trademarked in-game mechanics integrated through the Evolution distribution network [#2].

## [Primary Category: game-providers] Online Slot Games Supplied to Operators Worldwide

Nolimit City functions as a B2B iGaming software provider, distributing its online slot games directly to licensed operators rather than solely through third-party aggregators [#1][#2]. Its B2B MGA licence [#1] and integration into Evolution's One Stop Shop platform [#2] enable operators to onboard its catalogue of high-volatility slots as part of Evolution's wider content offering.

Market entry has been driven by named commercial partnerships rather than a single global rollout:

- SuperSport — strategic partnership marking entry into the Croatian market [#3]
- Apollo Games — partnership enabling entry into the Czech Republic [#4]
- Evolution — simultaneous US launch across Connecticut, Delaware, Michigan, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and West Virginia [#2]

Nolimit City describes itself as an "award-winning software provider" [#4]; the sourced material does not name specific awards or issuing bodies, so this positioning should be read as a company claim rather than a verified accolade.

## [Secondary Category: game-developers] Proprietary Slot Design and xMechanics Engineering

Beyond distribution, Nolimit City functions as a game developer, with named specialists credited for its mathematical and product design. Per Lindheimer, Head of Product [#9], and a Head of Math credited as "Wai" [#10] are cited discussing the studio's approach to volatility and feature design, while Jonas Tegman is named as co-founder [#12].

The studio's output centres on Nolimit City xMechanics — a suite of trademarked features including xNudge®, xWays®, xSplit®, xBomb® and xRIP® — described as the mechanical building blocks reused and recombined across releases [#6]. These mechanics have also become a licensable asset: Nolimit City extended an xMechanic licensing deal with Sneaky Slots, allowing that studio to build games using Nolimit City xWays, xNudge and xBomb components [#5]. This licensing model, alongside the high-volatility slots design ethos, differentiates the company's development activity from pure content-supply operations.

## [Tag: xmechanics] The xMechanics Suite: xNudge, xWays and xSplit

Nolimit City markets a defined family of trademarked mechanics — xNudge®, xWays®, xSplit®, xBomb® and xRIP® — as reusable mathematical building blocks across its catalogue [#6]. Tombstone, released in 2019, was among the earliest titles built around xNudge®, with the studio's Head of Math describing a deliberate decision to push the mechanic's volatility rather than soften it [#10].

The mechanics have since extended beyond Nolimit City's own catalogue: an extended licensing deal with Sneaky Slots permits that studio to build titles using Nolimit City xWays, xNudge and xBomb components under licence [#5]. This licensing arrangement is presented in company communications as evidence that xMechanics function as a distinct, monetisable intellectual-property layer rather than purely internal design tooling [#5].

## [Tag: high-volatility-slots] High-Volatility Math Models and Maximum-Win Design

Nolimit City is described in its own market announcements as producing "high volatility slots with often outlandish themes" [#2]. Tombstone is cited with a simulated maximum win of 11,456 times the stake in its US market listing [#2], and the company's "Two Outlaw Legacies" release material references maximum-win multipliers reaching into the hundreds of thousands [#15].

In an interview, Head of Product Per Lindheimer discusses the deliberate mathematical work behind volatile sequels, noting the team resists simply raising maximum wins without adding new design logic [#9]. These figures and design rationales are company-published; the sources reviewed do not include independent verification of payout frequency, RTP, or third-party volatility audits.

## [Tag: licensed-platform] Licensed B2B Platform and Group Structure

Nolimit City operates as a licensed B2B iGaming software provider under Nolimit City Limited, licensed by the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA/B2B/299/2015) [#1]. The company's website and services sit within a wider Evolution-operated domain structure — playin.com — that also spans Big Time Gaming, Red Tiger, NetEnt and Sneaky Slots properties under shared terms of service [#13].

Group-level terms are governed by Evolution Malta Limited (registration C 48666) [#13], while site data processing is handled by Nolimit City Stockholm AB [#14]. This shared licensing and platform infrastructure reflects Nolimit City's position within Evolution Group rather than a standalone technical stack, though the sources do not detail separate national supplier licences beyond Malta.

## [Tag: gamification] Playin Community Platform, Rewards and Streamer Legends

Nolimit City's content sits on the Playin platform, which layers gamification and loyalty features onto game discovery. Players start with 1,000 Playin Coins, usable across the studio's titles, and can earn more through daily rewards and a "chocolate wheel" mechanic that can also award Mystery Chests [#7]. The platform also runs leaderboards, achievements and free-to-enter tournaments — one cited event carried a €6,000 prize pool and over 20,000 participants [#7].

Separately, the Streamer Legends programme recognises streamers who record 5, 10 or 20 "Max Wins" on Nolimit City slots with tiered custom plaques (Nolimit LEGEND, GOD and GOAT) [#8]. Together, these initiatives extend Nolimit City's community engagement beyond direct operator supply into influencer- and player-facing recognition.
