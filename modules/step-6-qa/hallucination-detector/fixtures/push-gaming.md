## Push Gaming — B2B Slot Provider for Licensed Casino Operators

Push Gaming is a B2B slot provider that designs, builds and distributes online slots to licensed casino operators across regulated markets [^1][^15]. A London-founded slot game studio, the company supplies Push Gaming slots to a broad roster of tier-one operators and aggregators, positioning itself as an online casino game provider rather than a single-brand studio [^1]. Its catalogue is built around chart-topping titles such as Jammin' Jars, Razor Shark, Fat Rabbit and The Shadow Order, many of which are engineered specifically for mobile play [^15][^5].

Beyond individual game production, Push Gaming has broadened its scope into supplier-agnostic jackpot infrastructure with PrizeFlex™ and, through acquiring GSI, its own proprietary content-delivery platform [^8][^10]. The company markets its slots to operators in the UK, Malta, Sweden, the Netherlands, Ontario, Brazil and other regulated jurisdictions [^8][^5]. It emphasises a "Players First" ethos and responsible game design as core business values [^1].

The company describes its differentiation through proprietary in-game mechanics — Cluster Link™, Push-Up™ and Push Bet™ — that recur across its portfolio and drive repeat player engagement [^26]. Publicly disclosed financial detail and total headcount are limited in the available sources. What distinguishes Push Gaming is its dual identity: a slot game studio with a recognisable mechanic-led catalogue that has also built its own jackpot engine and delivery platform, supplying both content and infrastructure to the operators it serves [^8][^10].

## PrizeFlex™ Jackpot Solution Provider Infrastructure

Push Gaming's jackpot solution, PrizeFlex™, is a supplier-agnostic jackpot infrastructure product that can integrate with any game, casino or gambling product regardless of the originating provider [^8]. Launched in 2025, it extends the company's role from game production into standalone jackpot infrastructure that operators can layer across their content, establishing Push Gaming as a jackpot solution provider beyond its slot catalogue [^8].

The company describes its Push Gaming jackpot solution as offering:

- **Flexible jackpot types** — single-tier daily jackpots or multi-tier progressives, configurable per operator [^8].
- **Advanced contribution handling** — up to 250 contributions per second, with configurable contribution models [^8].
- **Market-specific configuration** — smart math models and adjustable RTP settings tailored to target markets [^8].
- **Cross-product application** — the open jackpot system can be applied to casino, Sportsbook, Bingo and Poker [^8].

According to the company, players can opt in and out of the side-bet jackpot at any time without impacting a title's base RTP, and no re-certification is required when configuring games to benefit from PrizeFlex™ [^8]. The system is described as live across eight jurisdictions — Brazil, Denmark, Malta, the Netherlands, Ontario, Romania, Sweden and the UK — having launched with LeoVegas, which the company states supports the world's largest online casino jackpot value [^8].
