## [Overview] Push Gaming — UK-Founded Online Slot Provider and B2B Gaming Supplier

Push Gaming is a UK-founded online slot provider and B2B gaming supplier that designs, produces and distributes slot content to regulated online casino operators [#1]. Founded in 2010, the company began by adapting land-based game concepts into online titles before shifting toward mobile-first HTML5 development from 2015 onward [#1][#3]. It operates from offices in London, Malta and Ukraine [#1], and its games — including Jammin' Jars, Razor Shark, Fat Rabbit and Joker Troupe — reach players through operator brands such as GVC, William Hill, bet365 and PokerStars [#7][#8][#9][#10].

As a B2B gaming supplier, Push Gaming combines content creation with platform technology. Following its 2020 acquisition of Game Server Integrations (GSI), it operates its own remote gaming server, Hive, and integration layer, MESH, alongside a UK Game Host licence and a Malta B2B licence [#2][#5]. The company transitioned "from boutique game developer to the ranks of full-service supplier" [#2], a shift marked by its 2020 rebrand [#3].

Push Gaming prioritizes quality over volume, releasing a smaller number of higher-quality titles annually rather than pursuing rapid, high-frequency launches [#1][#5]. The material reviewed does not identify a published total game count, employee headcount, or public pricing structure, leaving procurement detail for direct enquiry [#33].

## [Primary Category: game-developers] Game Design and Development as an Online Slots Developer

Push Gaming's product teams design games in-house, combining mathematics, art and mechanics rather than licensing third-party templates [#1][#4]. Product owners work with mathematicians and artists to prototype mechanics before development proceeds [#3].

**Proprietary Game Mechanics**

Push Gaming has developed distinct mechanics including Hypermode™, a timer-based feature introduced in Joker Troupe that speeds up reel spins as players land bonus symbols [#4], and the Nudging Stack Mechanic used in Razor Shark [#1]. Notable Push Gaming online slots include Jammin' Jars, Razor Shark, Wild Swarm, Fat Rabbit, Joker Troupe and Mystery Museum [#1][#4]. Titles such as Mount Magmas Jackpots and Crystal Catcher have been released as network jackpot content in partnership with LeoVegas [#11].

Product owner Amit Samji has stated the studio follows "a quality over quantity approach," releasing a limited number of games annually [#1]. The CEO referenced a 2020 target of up to ten new game releases [#5], though the material reviewed does not confirm the outcome against that target.

## [Secondary Category: casino-platforms] Proprietary Platform and B2B Gaming Supplier Infrastructure

Push Gaming's move into casino platforms followed its acquisition of Game Server Integrations (GSI) in early 2020, giving the company its own remote gaming server, Hive, and integration layer, MESH [#5]. The acquisition followed roughly a year of licensing the technology from GSI before the two businesses merged [#5].

**Direct Platform Control**

Operating its own platform lets Push Gaming manage technology development, market selection and delivery schedules directly with operators, rather than solely through aggregators [#2][#5]. The platform received a Game Host licence from the UK Gambling Commission and a B2B licence from the Malta Gaming Authority in January 2020 [#2]. Content is certified to the technical requirements of Sweden, Denmark, Latvia and Estonia, with support for Gibraltar, Alderney and the Isle of Man [#2].

Deals with GVC and Rootz's Wildz Online Casino are delivered via this proprietary platform [#7][#13], supporting Push Gaming's positioning as a B2B gaming supplier providing regulated market content across multiple territories [#2][#5].

## [Tag: licensed-platform] Regulated Market Content and Licensing Footprint

Push Gaming regulated market content also supports operations in Gibraltar, Alderney and the Isle of Man [#2]. The company has stated it sees "fantastic growth potential" in Spain, Latin America and Italy, though the material reviewed does not confirm these markets are yet live [#5].

## [Tag: software-development] In-House Platform and Integration Technology

Push Gaming's B2B gaming supplier capability rests on Hive, its remote gaming server, and MESH, its game integration layer, both acquired with GSI in 2020 [#5]. CEO James Marshall stated the studio initially licensed the technology for over a year before the businesses merged, after identifying "complementing teams and skill sets" [#5].

Owning this stack gives Push Gaming direct control over release cycles, market configuration and player-facing tools rather than dependence on external RGS providers [#5]. Distribution still runs partly through third-party integration partners such as Relax Gaming for several operator deals [#13][#14][#19].
