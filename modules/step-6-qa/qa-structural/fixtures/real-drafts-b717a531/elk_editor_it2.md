## [Overview] ELK Studios — Online Slots Provider for Licensed Operators

ELK Studios is an online slots provider founded in 2013 in Stockholm, Sweden [#4]. The company describes itself as the first Swedish game studio built with a clear focus on mobile platforms [#6], and it supplies premium slot content through hundreds of licensed operators and partners across regulated markets worldwide [#4]. Its catalogue spans dozens of named titles — from Wild Toro 3 to Pirots 5 and Quackers — built around proprietary mechanics such as CollectR™, avalanches and walking wilds [#3][#1].

As a slots and games provider, ELK Studios pairs concept design, in-house artwork and animation with statistical math models developed via cloud-based computing and big data [#4]. Beyond individual game titles, the company operates ELK Compete, a promotional engagement layer offering tournaments and challenges that operators can run on top of ELK's existing game library [#2]. The studio employs more than 100 people [#6] and holds gambling supply licences in Great Britain, Malta and Romania [#5], positioning it as a licensed supplier for operators across those jurisdictions.

Public sources do not detail ELK Studios' commercial terms, integration process, or geographic market reach beyond the licences held, leaving pricing and deployment specifics unconfirmed. ELK Studios' distinguishing characteristic is the combination of mobile-first slot design with named, trademarked in-house mechanics and a dedicated tournament/engagement product, rather than a broad multi-vertical content offering.

## [Secondary Category: gamification] ELK Compete: Promotional Engagement Tools for Operators

ELK Compete is ELK Studios' gamification product, adding tournaments, challenges and achievements on top of its existing slot catalogue [#2]. Operators configure multiplayer tournaments by setting the game, minimum bet level, tournament length and achievement structure, so players compete against each other over a defined period [#2].

The tool also supports individual challenges, where players work toward specific in-game goals — such as triggering respin features in Visitors or knocking down matadors in Wild Toro — with configurable milestones, spin limits or achievement thresholds [#2]. ELK Compete supports player segmentation, letting operators open a promotion to all players or invite selected segments via a promotional code or uploaded player list [#2]. As promotional engagement tools for operators, ELK Compete extends ELK Studios' offering beyond individual game supply into configurable, operator-run engagement campaigns built around ELK's own titles [#2].
